IOS 27

June 8, 2026

iOS 27 was unveiled at Apple's Worldwide Developers Conference on June 8, 2026, as the successor to iOS 26. Its headline feature is Siri AI, a rebuilt version of Siri built on generative AI models capable of holding natural, back-and-forth conversations. The update reached the public in September 2026 alongside the new iPhone lineup.

iPhone 17e

March 2, 2026

Announced March 2, 2026, and released March 11, the iPhone 17e is Apple's budget model, priced at $599. It upgrades on the iPhone 16e with the A19 chip, the faster C1X modem, and — for the first time in this lineup — MagSafe wireless charging.

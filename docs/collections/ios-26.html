IOS 26

June 9, 2025

iOS 26 was announced at WWDC on June 9, 2025 and released to the public on September 15, 2025. It was the first iOS version to carry a year-based number instead of a sequential one, aligning it with macOS, watchOS and Apple's other platforms, and introduced the Liquid Glass design language.

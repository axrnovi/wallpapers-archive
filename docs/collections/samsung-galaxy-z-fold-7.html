Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 7

July 9, 2025

Announced July 9, 2025, at Samsung's Unpacked event in Brooklyn and released July 25, the Galaxy Z Fold 7 became Samsung's thinnest and lightest book-style foldable to date. It was also the first Samsung foldable to feature a 200-megapixel main camera.

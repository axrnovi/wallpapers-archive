Mac OS X 10.6 Snow Leopard

June 9, 2008

Mac OS X 10.6, codenamed Snow Leopard, was announced at WWDC on June 9, 2008, more than a year before it arrived on August 28, 2009. Unlike most releases, it focused on refinement rather than new features, with Apple rewriting much of the operating system's core for better performance, stability, and a smaller storage footprint. It was also the first version sold exclusively as a digital download alongside physical media.
